# 108 formation-badge-ink-from-the-bloom
epic: none · pr: none

## Decisions
- @cards @color — A TextMeshPro material's ink is transcribed as the colour the game's camera puts on screen, not as the colour the material stores. The MainUI camera renders to an HDR buffer and its only volume component is a Bloom, so a face colour whose premultiplied value exceeds the bloom threshold reaches the user as a clipped core inside a spread glow, and the stored value is not a colour anyone ever sees. REJECTED: `clamp01(rgb * alpha)` for every material — it is right for the materials that stay under the threshold and wrong for exactly the ones the artists made HDR on purpose, which is the case a reader is most likely to check. REJECTED: carrying the HDR triple into CSS unchanged — CSS has no channel above one, so the clamp happens anyway, just without the glow that justified the value.
- @cards @color — The formation order number's two materials are split by where their premultiplied colours fall against the bloom threshold, and only the state above it carries a glow layer. `Participated` premultiplies to (2.248, 0.624, 0) and blooms; `Baton`'s face, and both underlays, stay under the threshold and are exact clamps. Treating the two alike would either invent a glow the game does not draw or drop the one it does. REJECTED: one glow for both states, tuned by eye — it makes the backup number wrong in the one state whose colour is derivable exactly, to spare the deployed state a second table entry.
- @cards @color (taste) — The bloom's spread is one measured radius, and its identifier says so. A six-iteration upsample pyramid has no closed form at the glyph core, so the attenuation that sets the core's colour and the radius that sets the glow's reach are fitted against the game's own capture while every other number in the chain is derived. REJECTED: deriving the radius from the pyramid's iteration count — the count bounds the spread but the visible falloff is set by the scatter and the blend, and the bound overstates it several times over.
- @cards @color — A colour read off a downscaled capture of the game is corrected against a material that is known not to bloom before it is trusted. Small saturated glyphs lose chroma in such a capture, which lifts the weak channel and reads as a colour shift that is not there; a same-capture glyph whose colour is derivable exactly gives the size of that error. REJECTED: sampling the capture and fitting straight to it — it moves colours that are already exact, in the direction the artefact points.
- @cards @color — A TextMeshPro underlay maps to CSS as a ring of copies at `_UnderlayDilate - _UnderlaySoftness` carrying a blur of `_UnderlaySoftness`, so the underlay's visible reach is the dilate alone. The dilate is how far the underlay's edge is pushed out and the softness is the width of the alpha ramp at that edge; `text-shadow` has no spread, and a blur reaches its own radius past the offset it is drawn at, so the ring may carry only what the blur does not. REJECTED: a ring at the dilate with a blur of the softness on every layer — it reads the two fields as independent when they share one edge, doubles the reach wherever a material softens its underlay, and measures on the order number as a rim 1.85x the game's own.

## Takeaway
- takeaway: an authored value and a displayed value are different quantities whenever anything sits between them; transcribe the one the user sees, and keep a control in frame to size the measurement's own error.
