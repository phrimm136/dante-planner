# 159 name-plates-draw-their-client-sprites
epic: none · pr: none · supersedes: 156 @nameplate

## Decisions
- @skill @passive @nameplate — Each name plate draws its own client sprite, sliced horizontally into a fixed left cap, a stretched centre and a fixed right cap at the sprite's borders: a skill plate is `BattleUI_CoinPopup_skillName` 69.33 units tall (caps 11.25 and 123.75 units) with its white body multiplied by the attribute colour, a passive plate is `New_MainUI_PersonalityList_2_46` 72.74 units tall (caps 112 and 171 units) multiplied by the client's dark text colour #a16a3b, which the passive box sets over the prefab's white, or by its importance level's panel colour. The name inside keeps its own node (skill 0–45 with margins 20/50, passive 25–40 with margins 30/100), and both names centre on their plate by TextMeshPro's geometry rule. The shared passive shape put a skill name's plate 14 units deeper than the sprite's body and drew a gradient tail whose dark part sat 75 to 90 units left of the sprite's, so names looked raised and the tail began under the name's last glyphs. REJECTED: re-deriving the gradient tail from the sprite — a hand transcription of a picture the client ships, which drifted once already; REJECTED: one shared plate shape — the two prefabs differ in sprite, height, size range and margins, and the phenomena came from sharing them.
  INVARIANT: no plate colour, tail or height is transcribed into gradients — plate style tests read the sprite pieces and their per-kind sizes.
- @skill @passive @nameplate — A tinted plate multiplies only the sprite's colour channels by the tint, through an SVG colour-matrix filter in sRGB, and each sprite ships as the whole-texel cover of its client rect (the skill plate's 148.5 × 25.5 texels as a 149 × 26 file whose extra half texel falls outside the box). The client's image shader multiplies texture colour by the tint and paints nothing where the texture is transparent, and the rect's half texel is part of what it stretches into the box. REJECTED: a tint background colour blended by multiply under a sprite mask — a pixel of alpha a paints a·d·c + (1 − a)·c, so every soft edge and the shadow rows gained a teal fringe the game does not show; REJECTED: the trimmed 148 × 25 export — the body started 1.4 units high and the texel pitch stretched.

## Takeaway
- takeaway: when the client draws an element from a sprite, ship the sprite; a transcription of its shape is a second source that drifts from the first.
